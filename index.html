Three steps to a story

Simple rules.
Unexpected answers.

Luminaria is best with 3–10 people. Every round, a new player becomes the storyteller.

01

Make a clue

The storyteller chooses one private card and gives it a short, imaginative clue.

02

Add your card

Everyone else secretly adds one card that could fit the clue.

03

Find the secret

Guess the storyteller’s card, and choose yours to fool the others.

✦
A little scoring twist

The storyteller scores when at least one, but not every, player finds their card. The best clues are unexpected.

Before you begin

A few answers
for the table.

How many people can play?

A real game starts with 3 players and supports up to 10. Three is the smallest table where bluffing and guessing stay interesting.

How long does a game last?

Each player spends one card every round. The number of rounds is calculated from the deck and player count before the game begins.

Do we need to create accounts?

No. Choose a nickname, create a room, and share its link. Guest sessions keep the table in sync while you play.

How Luminaria works

  1. One player gives a clue for their secret card.
  2. Everyone else plays a card that could fit the clue.
  3. Guess the storyteller’s card, and choose yours to fool the others.
✦

The storyteller gets 0 points if nobody — or everybody — finds the secret card. Choose an unexpected clue.
